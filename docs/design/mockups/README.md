## Detailed Style Breakdown

### 1. Style 1: Electric Cyan (`#00F0FF`) — "Aegis Pure Synthesis"
![Style 1: Electric Cyan UI](/home/josh/.gemini/antigravity-cli/brain/108e4a49-2e69-4bd1-aa94-1047f8176c0a/mockup_cyan_peptides_1789083927340.jpg)

- **Palette Foundation:** Void Black (`#090B0E`) with luminous Electric Cyan (`#00F0FF`) and subtle lavender accents.
- **3D Render Staging:** 10ml clear borosilicate glass serum vial with an anodized electric cyan crimp seal, containing a sterile freeze-dried lyophilized cake on an elevated dark pedestal with cyan rim lighting.
- **Data & Trust Elements:** High-contrast `≥99.4% HPLC PURITY` badge, chemical formula, and high-visibility Cyan CTA.
- **Best Suited For:** Peptides (BPC-157, TB-500, CJC-1295) and primary brand interface elements.

---

### 2. Style 2: Synaptic Emerald (`#00FF9D`) — "Bio-Intelligence & Synaptic Mint"
![Style 2: Synaptic Emerald UI](/home/josh/.gemini/antigravity-cli/brain/108e4a49-2e69-4bd1-aa94-1047f8176c0a/mockup_emerald_nootropics_1789083941208.jpg)

- **Palette Foundation:** Deep charcoal slate (`#0B0E14`) with intense neon synth green / emerald (`#00FF9D`).
- **3D Render Staging:** Frosted matte black dropper bottle alongside solid capsule vials, illuminated by radiant green caustic ground reflections.
- **Data & Trust Elements:** Monospace specifications block, cognitive categorization tag, and sharp glowing mint CTA.
- **Best Suited For:** Nootropics (Phenylpiracetam, Alpha-GPC, Citicoline, L-Theanine) and high-purity laboratory certification tags.

---

### 3. Style 3: Hyper Amber (`#FFB300`) — "Metabolic Affinity & Solar Gold"
![Style 3: Hyper Amber UI](/home/josh/.gemini/antigravity-cli/brain/108e4a49-2e69-4bd1-aa94-1047f8176c0a/mockup_amber_sarms_1789083954295.jpg)

- **Palette Foundation:** Pitch obsidian (`#080A0E`) with vibrant solar gold / warm amber (`#FFB300`).
- **3D Render Staging:** Pharmaceutical serum vial with an anodized gold cap, warm amber rim lighting tracing the silhouette, resting on a textured carbon composite pedestal.
- **Data & Trust Elements:** Monospace CAS identifier (`CAS 1182367-47-0`), amber pill RUO tag (`FOR RESEARCH USE ONLY`), and glowing amber CTA.
- **Best Suited For:** SARMs (RAD-140, Ostarine, LGD-4033, Cardarine) and advisory / RUO compliance notices.

---

### 4. Style 4: Hyper-Violet (`#A855F7`) — "Elune Amethyst Night"
![Style 4: Hyper-Violet UI](/home/josh/.gemini/antigravity-cli/brain/108e4a49-2e69-4bd1-aa94-1047f8176c0a/mockup_violet_elune_1789083969494.jpg)

- **Palette Foundation:** Deep velvet matte black with electric hyper-violet and neon purple (`#A855F7`, `#C084FC`).
- **3D Render Staging:** Serum vial with metallic purple crimp seal casting vibrant caustic ripples onto an obsidian slab.
- **Data & Trust Elements:** Tabbed scientific interface (`Description`, `Usage`, `Lab Results`, `Specification`), molecular weight callout, and violet neon CTA glow.
- **Best Suited For:** The brand's signature master theme and high-end peptide blends.
